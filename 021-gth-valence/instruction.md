In CP2K with Goedecker–Teter–Hutter (GTH) pseudopotentials, the potential label **`GTH-PBE-q6`** on oxygen indicates how many **valence electrons** are explicitly treated for that atom (the `-qN` suffix).

For an oxygen atom specified with **`GTH-PBE-q6`**, write **only** the integer valence electron count to `/app/out.txt`.
